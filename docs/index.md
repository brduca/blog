# Watercolor Dreams — Coming Soon

*Composing the soundtrack of a life.*

**Watercolor Dreams** is an immersive journey through soundscapes where music flows like wet paint across paper. In this album, Duca translates dreams and memories into ethereal textures, blending the organic warmth of stringed instruments with subtle ambient atmospheres. 

Each track acts as a delicate brushstroke, painting musical landscapes that invite the listener to drift through shifting tones and quiet moments. It is the Soundtrack to an introspective escape.
